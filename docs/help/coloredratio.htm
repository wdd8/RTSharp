The colored ratio plugin makes the Ratio column of the listing colored, so you can see at a glance which torrents need some more seeding. There is nothing to configure, just add an instance of the plugin and restart RT#.

- Ratio below 1: Red, the lower the ratio, the brighter the red
- Ratio between 1 and 5: Green, the higher the ratio, the brighter the green
- Ratio between 5 and 25: From purple to dark, on a black background
- Higher ratios: White on a black background
