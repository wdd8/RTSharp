Labels are a simple way to organize your torrents, a torrent can have as many labels as you want and they are stored by the torrent daemon itself, so they are kept even if you use another client.

To add a label, select the torrents, right click on them and open the "Set label..." menu. This menu displays all the labels used by your torrents with a check mark when the selected torrents have this label. Click on a label to add it to the selected torrents, or to remove it if it is already checked.

To create a new label, click on "Add..." at the bottom of the menu, type the name of the label and press Enter. The label is added to all the selected torrents.

Once your torrents are labeled, you can use the funnel of the Labels column to display only the torrents with some labels. Also the search as you go looks at the Labels column, so typing the beginning of the first label of a torrent is enough to find it.
