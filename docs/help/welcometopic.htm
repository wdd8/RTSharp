RT# (also called RTSharp) is designed for people who want to manage their torrents on one or several remote servers from a single place. For example, a server can run hundreds or thousands of torrents and to find the right torrent you have to open the web interface of each torrent client one by one, each of them having its own look, its own shortcuts and its own limitations. RT# includes powerful features that will help you manage all your torrents in all your servers.

A torrent setup is usually made of several torrent daemons. RT# works with rtorrent, qBittorrent and Transmission at the same time and shows all their torrents in one single listing.

RT# is made of two parts: the RT# application that runs on your computer (Windows, Linux or macOS) and the RTSharp.Daemon service that runs on each Linux server, next to your torrent daemons. Both are talking to each other through an encrypted connection where each side is identified by its certificate.

It also includes a plugin system that allows you to add new features or to support new torrent daemons, and a scripting interface that runs your own scripts directly on the servers.
