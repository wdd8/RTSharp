The log

The "Log" tab displays all the messages of the application, with their date and time, their level (Information, Warning, Error...) and their text. When something does not work as expected, this is the first place to look at. If a message comes with an error, right click on it and select "Copy exception" to copy the full error details to the clipboard, this is very useful when you report a problem.

The daemon logs

RT# can also display the logs of your torrent daemons. In the data providers pane, right click on a data provider and select "Logs", a new window will open with the recent lines of the log followed by the new lines as soon as they are written. For each line, the window displays its date and time, its severity and its message. Click on "Clear" to empty the window.

For rtorrent and Transmission, the daemon must know where the log file is: set the LogFile setting of the data provider in the daemon configuration, as explained in the "Data provider configuration" chapter.
