The data providers pane is located on the right side of the main window and gives you a quick overview of all your torrent daemons. For each data provider, RT# displays:

- The color of the data provider, as a thin bar on the left
- The name of the data provider instance
- An icon representing its state (connected, disconnected...) and the latency to its server
- The number of active torrents in a blue bubble
- The total download and upload speeds
- A speed chart of the last minutes

The speed chart is scaled automatically on the highest speed of the period, this maximum speed is displayed in its top right corner. If you don't need the chart, you can hide it with the "Show speed chart" option in the Look options.

Right click on a data provider and select "Logs" to display the logs of its torrent daemon, as explained in the next chapters. This item is only available when the daemon is able to stream its logs.
