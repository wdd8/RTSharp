Browse

Using RT#'s user interface you will easily be able to browse all your torrents, even if they are spread across several servers and several torrent daemons. The torrent listing can be sorted on several columns, filtered and searched just by typing the beginning of a name, giving you fast access even with tens of thousands of torrents.

Control

Just select one or several torrents and you can start, pause, stop, recheck, reannounce, label or remove them in one click, whatever the torrent daemon they belong to. RT# will send each action to the right server and will display its progress in the action queue. If a torrent daemon does not support an action, the corresponding menu item is disabled.

Inspect

Click on a torrent and you will get detailed information about its transfer, its files, its peers and its trackers. The peers are displayed with their country and the company that owns their network, the trackers with their own icon and name. You can even get the mediainfo of a file or preview it with mpv while it is still on the server.

Move and duplicate

RT# allows you to move the data of your torrents to another directory of the server, but also to duplicate a torrent and its data to another torrent daemon, on the same server or on a completely different server. The data is transferred directly from server to server, it never goes through your computer.

Create

RT# includes a torrent creator that supports V1, V2 and hybrid torrents with performance usually limited by disk I/O.

Plugins and scripts

RT# can be extended with plugins that add menus, columns colors, windows or even new torrent daemons. Also the servers can run C# scripts that you write yourself, either once or permanently as a service.

Statistics

RT# displays the total speed of each torrent daemon with a speed chart, the global statistics of your listing and the all-time statistics of the daemons. Each server can also expose its tracker statistics to Prometheus.
